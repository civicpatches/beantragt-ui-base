# Brand Assets

The platform fallback uses the beantragt.ai logo and the Civic Patches producer logo. Each has a light-background SVG and a `-dark.svg` variant for dark backgrounds. Operators can replace these assets or hide the producer link with an empty `NUXT_PUBLIC_CIVIC_PATCHES_URL`. A municipality supplies its own branding through its backend configuration.

`beantragt-mark.svg` and `beantragt-mark-dark.svg` show the symbol without the wordmark in the citizen footer. They contain the three `mark-line` paths and the `mark-dot` from the corresponding logo, with a viewBox fitted to the symbol. Keep the geometry and colors in sync when updating the logos.
